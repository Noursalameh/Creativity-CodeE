About Creativity Code


At creativity Code , we bring imagination to life. Based in Jordan, our team specializes in transforming spaces through creative design and the innovative use of WPC — a material that combines the warmth of wood with the durability of modern composites.


We believe that great design is not only about aesthetics, but also about sustainability and functionality. With every project, we aim to create environments that inspire, comfort, and endure.

Where Craftsmanship Meets Innovation

Premier WPC Solutions Across Jordan

Project 01

Guest House Petra

Supply and installation of complete WPC pergola systems for guest rooms and restaurant areas. A fully integrated solution designed to enhance comfort and functionality, providing durable shading and a refined outdoor experience that complements Petra’s unique architectural character.

Hospitality Pergola Systems Heritage Site
Project 02

The Blessed Tree – Ministry of Awqaf

A sacred mission requiring reverent craftsmanship. We provided synthetic wood cladding for the protective metal frame surrounding Jordan's holy Blessed Tree. This spiritually significant project combines cutting-edge material technology with deep respect for religious heritage, preserving this landmark for future generations while maintaining natural aesthetics.

Religious Site Heritage Protection Sacred Architecture
Project 03

Chalet Teresa

Supply and installation of shading systems, exterior facades, and outdoor works. A complete outdoor solution designed to enhance aesthetics, privacy, and durability. The project blends modern exterior finishes with functional shading elements to create a comfortable and visually refined chalet environment.

Outdoor Solutions Residential Premium WPC
Project 04

German Jordanian University

Supply and installation of WPC cladding over existing steel canopies across campus walkways and outdoor spaces. The solution delivers long-lasting weather protection, modern appearance, and low-maintenance performance tailored to the university’s contemporary architectural style.

Education Canopy Cladding WPC Systems
Project 05

Qasr Al-Hallabat Palace

Supply and installation of elegant WPC entrance canopies for this historic Umayyad desert palace. The design harmoniously blends modern materials with ancient architecture, offering durable shading while preserving the site’s cultural and archaeological integrity.

Heritage Site Entrance Canopy Restoration
Project 06

United Nations Facility

Supply and installation of premium WPC solutions for outdoor areas, providing sustainable, weather-resistant structures that meet international standards. A reliable and aesthetically refined enhancement for functional and representative spaces.

International Organization WPC Structures Sustainable Design
Project 07

Al-Mashreq Schools

Supply and installation of shaded benches, tables, and canopy systems using durable WPC materials. Creating comfortable and safe outdoor learning and recreation spaces designed to withstand heavy student use and Jordan’s climate.

Education Outdoor Furniture Shaded Areas
Project 08

Royal Diving Club

Supply and installation of a marine pier constructed entirely from durable WPC (Wood Plastic Composite). Engineered to withstand harsh saltwater conditions, this elevated walkway provides safe and elegant access over the Red Sea, blending functionality with coastal aesthetics.

Marine WPC Decking Coastal Project

Why Choose Our Products

Why you need to know about WPC?

WPC is an innovative eco-friendly material combining wood fibers and recycled plastic for exceptional durability and natural aesthetics.

Superior Performance

Our WPC products are UV-resistant, water-resistant, and termite-proof, making them ideal for both indoor and outdoor applications.

Sustainable Choice

WPC products are sustainable and help reduce deforestation while providing the beauty of natural wood with enhanced performance.
